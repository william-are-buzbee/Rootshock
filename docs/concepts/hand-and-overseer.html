The Hand and the Overseer

Four of each, built from the same boxes, icosahedra and six-sided cylinders as the rest of the cast, in its colours and lit the way it is lit. The husk and the thresher (today's hand) stand by for scale, with a doorway: 2 m wide, 2.4 m high.

Where I'd go

Hand: the Stack. It is the only one that could only be the response team: a breaching stack is how they moved through doors, and now it is how they move. Long and low, it turns badly, so the hall's pillars and benches do something, and the shields at its front are wider than any doorway, which explains the door rule on sight. The Line is the runner-up; its marching in step is the best sound cue of the four.

Overseer: the Operator. "Not a boss, a seat", taken at its word. The chair turning is a tell you can hear, and from the hall its head over the sill shows you which way it faces. The Periscope does the same job with a better silhouette from below; the two could be combined.

What they would cost

  • All eight use only mk/grp and the cast's palette, so each ports to castView.ts as one BUILD and one ANIM entry. The tell and blow here are driven by a phase cycle shaped like blowAt.
  • The Stack and the Line want a different body than the thresher's 0.65 m circle: a capsule or a short chain of circles for the Stack, a wide box for the Line. The Engorged and the Cordon fit a bigger circle.
  • The overseers keep today's split: the watchers and cords stay level geometry (drawn here for context), the body is the cast model.
  • None of these is a hand.
